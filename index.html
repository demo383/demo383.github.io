The Krishna story

From a home kitchen to a community table.

Krishna Catering & Restaurant was established in 1994 by Manu and Ila Patel, growing from a love of cooking at home and at the temple into a neighborhood destination for vegetarian Indian food.

Today, the menu brings together North and South Indian favorites, Gujarati and Punjabi dishes, street-style snacks, and Indo-Chinese fusion — with options for Jain and vegan diners.
